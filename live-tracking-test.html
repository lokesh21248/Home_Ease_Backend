      How Live Tracking Works Under the Hood
    

    

      

        1. Worker Ingestion (Port 8082)
        Worker app connects to STOMP /ws and emits coordinates to /app/tracking/{bookingId} every 3-5s (or uses fallback REST POST /api/v1/tracking/update-location).
      

      

        2. Redis Geospatial Index
        Spring Boot microservice records the point in Redis GEO (geo:partners) and caches in tracking:booking:{bookingId} with 2-hr TTL.
      

      

        3. Customer Fanout
        The broker broadcasts the payload instantly to /topic/tracking/{bookingId}. Customer app receives it with 0ms polling delay and smoothly rotates the marker.
